# DropMarket — Concierge Outreach Kit (the 20 warm applicants)

You have ~20 people who applied to be founding sellers and ~3 who joined Discord. That's a
small, named, *warm* list — the exact situation where **1:1 concierge beats any funnel**
(see the seller-acquisition plan). Don't blast them. Reach each one like a person.

**The wedge is always SafeDrop, not fees.** A trader's #1 fear is "go first and get scammed."
Lead with "you get paid even if the buyer bails" — fees are a distant second.

---

## Where your list lives

Admin → **Founding Sellers** (`/admin/early-sellers`). Every applicant with their email,
Discord (if given), what they said they sell, and status. Export CSV there if you want it in
a sheet.

Two ways to reach them:
- **The Founding HQ invite email** — one click per row ("Send HQ Invite" ✈️ button), or
  "Invite New (N)" to email everyone still marked *New*. This sends them their personalized HQ
  link automatically. Good for the *first* touch at scale.
- **A hand-written 1:1 message** (below) — for anyone who gave a Discord handle, or as a
  follow-up. This is what actually converts the fence-sitters.

Best sequence: **HQ invite email first** (gets everyone their link), then **hand-written
follow-up** 2–3 days later to anyone who hasn't set up their account.

---

## Rules of the concierge motion

- **Reference their actual item.** They told you what they sell in the form — use it. "Saw you
  trade Adopt Me pets" beats "Dear seller." It's a pattern-interrupt and proves you're real.
- **One follow-up, then stop.** Never spam. One nudge is fine; two is annoying.
- **Reply in-thread where you can; DM only where it's welcome.** For the 3 in Discord, message
  them there. Don't cold-DM people in servers you don't own (ToS ban).
- **Offer to do the work.** "Send me the item + a screenshot, I'll build and price the listing
  for you." Removing effort is the whole game at this stage.
- **Never ask them to go first.** Ever. That's the scam pattern they're scared of.

## What to track (ignore vanity numbers)

Not "how many signups." Track the funnel that matters:

1. **DM/email → reply rate** (are the messages landing?)
2. **Reply → account created** (did they get through setup?)
3. **Account → listing live** (the north star: real listings from real founders)
4. **First completed sale** (the proof that unlocks everything — triggers the Trustpilot invite)

20 people, worked by hand, is a week or two of real conversations — not a campaign. A handful
of activated founding sellers with live listings is worth more than 500 cold signups.

---

## The honest priorities (from the research)

- **One game.** Concentrate on whichever game your data shows most demand (likely Steal a
  Brainrot or Adopt Me). A store that looks alive in one category beats 18 half-empty ones.
- **Consignment, not buying inventory.** Seller keeps the item, you list it, ownership moves on
  sale. Never buy/warehouse currency yourself (that makes you an RMT vendor — publisher ban risk).
- **Fund a few first sales** (time-boxed, ~1 per seller, small ceiling) so early sellers feel a
  real payout. That's what breaks the "no buyers yet" deadlock.
