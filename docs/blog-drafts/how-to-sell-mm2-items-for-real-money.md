## MM2 Rarities Explained
Buyers filter by rarity first. This is roughly what each one is worth.

| Rarity | What it is | Typical price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common to Legendary | Unboxed from crates | Under $1 |
| **Godly** | Rare box drops and event items | Under $1 to $120 |
| **Ancient** | Event pass rewards and rare drops | Under $1 to $320 |
| **Vintage** | Items from MM2's early years | Varies a lot |
| **Chroma** | Rainbow version of a godly | $1 to $3,000+ |

> Rare doesn't always mean valuable. Some old items have almost no buyers. Always check live prices before you list.

## How to Sell MM2 Items on DropMarket
:::steps
Go to the Sell page
Choose Murder Mystery 2, then Items
Pick your item (normal or Chroma)
Set your price from live listings
Choose your delivery time
Publish your listing
:::

### Pricing tips that sell faster
- **Pick the exact item.** A Chroma listed as the normal version gets skipped by Chroma buyers.
- **Price just under similar listings.** Buyers sort by price.
- **Use a short delivery time** if you're online often. Buyers pay more for fast delivery.
- **List more than one item.** Sellers with more listings get more views.

## How You Get Paid
:::steps
A buyer purchases your item
You trade it to them in MM2 within your delivery time
The buyer confirms they got it
The money goes to your Seller Balance
You withdraw to your payout method
:::

Every order is covered by **SafeDrop Protection**. Buyers know they get their item or their money back, so they're happy to buy from new sellers. You can make your first sale with no reviews.

## Seller Tips to Protect Your Sales
- **Keep all chat in the order chat.** It's your proof if there's ever a problem.
- **Never sell outside DropMarket.** "Same deal, no fees" offers leave you with no protection.
- **Never share your Roblox password.** MM2 delivery is always a normal in-game trade.
- **Check the trade window before you accept.** Last-second item swaps are the most common MM2 scam.
- **List the exact item.** The wrong item or version means a refund.
